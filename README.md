# PT/OS One

**Your personal AI operating framework in plain text. As simple as it gets.**

## Stop starting from scratch with AI.

You've told your AI how you like things written. You've explained what you're working on, what matters to you, and how you prefer to get things done.

Then you open another AI assistant, start a new conversation, and find yourself explaining everything again.

**PT/OS One gives you a simple way to take that information with you.**

It's a personal document that helps AI assistants understand you, use the information you've chosen to preserve, and pick up where you left off.

You own it. You control what's in it. And you can use it with different AI assistants without rebuilding everything from scratch.

No special software. No complicated setup. No subscription required.

**Just one document.**

## What can you do with it?

PT/OS One can help your AI understand:

- **How you work.** Your preferences, communication style, and instructions.
- **What you're doing.** Your projects, goals, priorities, and unfinished work.
- **What you've decided.** Important decisions and the reasoning behind them.
- **What you do repeatedly.** Tasks and workflows you'd rather not explain every time.

You don't need to fill out a questionnaire or write a biography.

Start with something useful. Your AI helps you build the document through an ordinary conversation.

## Getting started

Copy this prompt into your favorite AI assistant:

```text
Help me set up PT/OS One using https://github.com/cooperman411/plain-text-os-one. Read the README.md and AGENT.md files, then walk me through creating my own personal AI document. Keep it simple and ask me one question at a time.
```

**That's it.** If your AI can open the repository, it should guide you through creating and saving your document.

### If your AI can't open the link

Some AI assistants cannot access GitHub directly. Open [AGENT.md](AGENT.md), copy its contents into your AI conversation, and say:

> Help me get started.

Your AI should still be able to guide you through setup. If it can create files, it may provide a document to download. Otherwise, it should give you the complete text to save yourself.

Once you've created your personal document, you won't need `AGENT.md` for ordinary use. Your document contains the instructions another AI needs to understand and maintain it.

## From simple conversations to more capable AI

Not all AI assistants can do the same things. PT/OS One is designed to work with that reality.

**Using a basic chat assistant?**

You can organize projects, track priorities, preserve decisions, develop ideas, and maintain your personal document. Everything can happen in an ordinary chat window.

**Using an AI with additional tools?**

Your assistant may also be able to search connected email, work with calendars, create files, execute workflows, or manage scheduled tasks.

PT/OS One helps the assistant understand what you want accomplished. The assistant uses whatever capabilities are actually available and authorized.

You don't need a more complicated version of PT/OS One to use those features. And if an assistant cannot perform something, it should tell you rather than suggest an impractical workaround.

For platform-specific details, see the [AI Compatibility Guide](CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md).

## Keeping it current

Your document isn't meant to become a historical archive of everything you've ever said to an AI. It's a practical reference that changes as your life and work change.

When something important changes, ask your assistant to update your PT/OS One. It should preserve information that's still useful, revise what's changed, and help you save the updated version.

**One important limitation:** PT/OS One doesn't automatically synchronize information between AI services. You'll need to provide the current document when switching assistants, unless they already have authorized access to it.

## Your information stays under your control

You decide what goes into your document, where to keep it, and which AI services can read it.

Avoid including passwords, account credentials, or other sensitive secrets. Remember that information you share with an online AI assistant is subject to that service's privacy practices.

PT/OS One is an open, plain-text approach. It doesn't lock your information into a particular application or platform.

## Start small. Make it useful.

You don't need to organize your entire life to get something out of PT/OS One.

Start with one project, one preference, or one thing you're tired of explaining. Your document can grow with you.

**The goal isn't to build another complicated system. It's to make the AI tools you already use more useful.**
